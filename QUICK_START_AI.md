# Quick Start: AI Tools in PRIME Teaching Platform

## How to Access AI Tools

### Method 1: From Dashboard
1. Log in to PRIME Teaching Platform
2. Click on the **"AI Tools"** card in the Quick Actions section
3. Choose your tool

### Method 2: From Sidebar
1. Log in to PRIME Teaching Platform  
2. Look for **"AI Tools"** in the left sidebar (with ✨ icon)
3. Click to see all 4 tools

### Method 3: Direct Links
- 🏫 Curriculum Breakdown: `/dashboard/ai-tools/curriculum`
- ⚡ Lesson Plan Generator: `/dashboard/ai-tools/lesson-generator`
- 📖 Teaching Notes: `/dashboard/ai-tools/teaching-notes`
- 🎓 Teacher Development: `/dashboard/ai-tools/teacher-development`

## 5-Minute Quick Start

### Generate Your First Lesson Plan (5 minutes)

1. **Go to Lesson Plan Generator**
   - Dashboard → AI Tools → Lesson Plan Generator

2. **Fill in the basic info**
   - Subject: `Mathematics`
   - Grade Level: `Grade 11`
   - Duration: `45` minutes

3. **Describe your resources**
   - Example: "Textbook chapter 5, projector, whiteboard, graphing calculators"

4. **Click Generate**
   - Wait 20-45 seconds

5. **Download and Use**
   - View the lesson plan
   - Click "Download" to save as JSON
   - Use it for your next class

## Tool Quick Reference

### 🏫 Curriculum Breakdown
**Takes:** Textbook content  
**Gives:** 10+ organized learning sessions  
**Time:** 5 minutes  
**Best For:** Planning an entire unit or chapter

**Example Input:**
```
Chapter 5: Quadratic Equations
5.1 What are quadratic equations
5.2 Solving by factoring
5.3 Using the quadratic formula
5.4 Applications and word problems
[... paste full chapter text ...]
```

---

### ⚡ Lesson Plan Generator
**Takes:** Resources + learning objectives  
**Gives:** Complete 45-minute lesson plan  
**Time:** 3 minutes  
**Best For:** Planning individual class sessions

**Example Input:**
```
Resources: Textbook section 3.2, online simulation, worksheet
Objectives: Students will understand exponential growth and decay
```

---

### 📖 Teaching Notes
**Takes:** Your lesson plan  
**Gives:** Instructor notes with strategies  
**Time:** 2 minutes  
**Best For:** Preparing to teach a lesson

**Use After:** Generating a lesson plan  
**Includes:** Common misconceptions, questioning strategies, differentiation

---

### 🎓 Teacher Development
**Takes:** Training materials  
**Gives:** Personalized 6-month growth plan  
**Time:** 2 minutes  
**Best For:** Professional development

**Example Input:**
```
Paste any professional development materials:
- Workshop notes
- Training articles
- Course materials
- Coaching feedback
```

## Typical Weekly Workflow

### Monday: Plan the Week
1. **Use Curriculum Breakdown** (if new unit)
   - Organize chapter into weekly lessons

2. **Generate Lesson Plans** (one per day)
   - Create 5 daily lesson plans

### Tuesday-Friday: Teach with Confidence
1. **Use Teaching Notes** (before each lesson)
   - Review misconceptions
   - Prepare questioning strategies
   - Plan accommodations

2. **Deliver Lessons**
   - Follow the generated lesson plan
   - Use teaching notes for reference
   - Monitor student understanding

### End of Month: Grow Professionally
1. **Reflect on Practice**
   - What went well?
   - What could improve?

2. **Use Teacher Development** (monthly)
   - Update your growth plan
   - Set next month's goals
   - Track progress

## Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Quality Input = Quality Output
- Paste detailed resources, not just titles
- Be specific about learning objectives
- Include grade level and subject

### Tip 2: Iteration Improves Results
- Generate multiple versions
- Customize the best one for your class
- Save your favorites

### Tip 3: Use for Differentiation
- Generate with "struggling learners" focus
- Generate with "advanced learners" focus
- Create multiple versions for mixed ability

### Tip 4: Download and Archive
- Save all generated content as JSON
- Create a resource library
- Reuse and adapt for future years

### Tip 5: Collaborate with Colleagues
- Download and share lesson plans
- Get feedback on generated content
- Create departmental resources

## Common Questions

### Q: How long does generation take?
**A:** 20 seconds to 2 minutes depending on complexity. Longer inputs take longer.

### Q: Can I edit the generated content?
**A:** Yes! Download it and customize for your specific classroom.

### Q: What if I don't like the output?
**A:** Generate again! Try different input or refine your prompt.

### Q: Can I use this for multiple classes?
**A:** Absolutely! Generate once, customize for each class.

### Q: Is my content secure?
**A:** Yes. Content is sent to OpenAI but not stored in our platform.
