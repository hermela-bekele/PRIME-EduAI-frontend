## ✨ The 4 AI Tools at a Glance

### 1️⃣ **Curriculum Breakdown** 🏫
Break down textbooks into organized learning sessions
- **Takes:** Textbook content + number of days
- **Gives:** Structured learning sessions with objectives
- **Time:** 30-60 seconds
- **Best for:** Planning entire units or chapters
- **Access:** `/dashboard/ai-tools/curriculum`

### 2️⃣ **Lesson Plan Generator** ⚡
Create comprehensive lesson plans in minutes
- **Takes:** Resources + learning objectives
- **Gives:** Complete 45-minute lesson plans
- **Time:** 20-45 seconds
- **Best for:** Planning individual class sessions
- **Access:** `/dashboard/ai-tools/lesson-generator`

### 3️⃣ **Teaching Notes** 📖
Generate instructor notes with strategies and misconceptions
- **Takes:** Lesson plan
- **Gives:** Comprehensive teaching notes
- **Time:** 45-90 seconds
- **Best for:** Preparing to teach a lesson
- **Access:** `/dashboard/ai-tools/teaching-notes`

### 4️⃣ **Professional Development** 🎓
Create personalized professional growth plans
- **Takes:** Training materials or workshop notes
- **Gives:** Personalized 6-month development plan
- **Time:** 60-120 seconds
- **Best for:** Professional growth and reflection
- **Access:** `/dashboard/ai-tools/teacher-development`
